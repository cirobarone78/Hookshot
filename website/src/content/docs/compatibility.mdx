## Requirements used

Checked against the [upstream requirements](https://github.com/NotRexed/Hookshot#what-you-need)
and [project status](https://github.com/NotRexed/Hookshot#project-status) on **November 10, 2026**.

The calculator is reviewed against the README's requirements and GPU status. A website build stops if either
source section changes, until the calculator has been reviewed again. The source links above contain the full requirements.

This checks the selected requirements, not performance or stability. Confirm the capabilities of your installed driver;
the checker does not maintain a driver-version database.
